Create a Search Results asset around one recognizable visual that adds something to your app's name and subtitle. In Mockup Kit on Mac, choose the Search Results placement, keep that visual inside the art-safe guide, and check it beside your listing details in the illustrative search preview before exporting.

Search Results artwork is optional and separate from required screenshots. Apple describes creative placements for eligible apps on iOS and iPadOS 27 and later. For the decision between a dedicated search image and one file for both placements, see the [Universal artwork guide](/blog/choose-universal-or-separate-app-store-creative-assets/). [Apple's asset guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/)

## Set up the search scene

1. Open Headers & Search Results and select Search Results. Start with an editable template or build a scene with your own artwork. Use a background that reaches the edges of the canvas.
2. Choose one subject that shows what the app does. Move and resize the subject inside the art-safe guide. Leave space around the details someone needs to recognize it.
3. Open the illustrative search preview and enter your intended app icon, name, and subtitle. Compare how the artwork and listing details read together. If a text layer repeats the subtitle, hide the layer and see whether the image is clearer.

Apple accepts opaque PNG or JPEG images at 3:2, from 1920 × 1280 to 3840 × 2560 pixels. At 3840 × 2560, its art-safe area is 2168 × 1030 pixels, starting at x = 836 and y = 765. Use the guide for your placement and check the applicable dimensions before exporting. [Apple's creative asset specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/creative-assets-specifications)

## Check what the image adds in search

Imagine an audio editor with the subtitle “Trim audio clips.” Try a visual with one waveform, a highlighted selection, and two trim handles. Hide a “Trim audio clips” text layer and inspect the search preview again. The subtitle names the task, and the handles show how it works. If the handles disappear at a glance, enlarge the selection and handles together inside the guide. Let a faint waveform texture fill the sides, where losing part of it would leave the example intact.

Read the preview once with the subtitle and once while looking only at the artwork. In the first pass, check that the image adds a detail the subtitle leaves out. In the second, check that you can still pick out the subject without reading any text on the image. Use the crop guides to confirm that the details doing that work remain inside the art-safe area.

## Export and open the file

Export an opaque PNG or JPEG and reopen it to check the dimensions and confirm that the important details remain legible. Preflight checks dimensions, formats, and missing content. The search preview is illustrative. Apple controls the final placement and reviews submitted artwork.

One selected screenshot or creative export is free. Premium unlocks unlimited selected exports and Export All. Projects remain available during the current session, so keep the source artwork and final export before quitting. Use the [product page Header guide](/blog/how-to-create-app-store-product-page-header/) if you are designing the wider companion image.
