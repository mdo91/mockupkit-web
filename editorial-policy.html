Editorial Policy

Mockup Kit guides are published by Damas Technologies, the app’s developer. Their purpose is to help app creators prepare accurate, readable artwork and understand the limits of the tool.

How guides are prepared

Automation and AI assistance may help research, organize, and draft guide content and metadata. Product claims are checked against the maintained product facts and app source; Apple requirements are linked to Apple’s own documentation. The repository’s tests check technical output, links, and metadata.

New and materially revised guides are prepared in a pull request. A separate automated editorial review checks the exact revision against maintained product facts, sources and existing coverage before publication, alongside website checks. Rejected or unverified guides remain held. Automated review is not human sign-off and does not prove a conversion result or the quality of a translation. An organization byline identifies the publisher; it does not claim that a named person has tested every example.

Sources and examples

Guides link relevant first-party sources for placement requirements. Mockup Kit screenshots and example copy illustrate workflows; they are not testimonials or evidence of a measured performance improvement. Upcoming features are labeled until their public release is verified.

Dates and corrections

Publication dates stay attached to the original article. An updated date records a significant correction or content change, rather than an automatic freshness refresh. App Store specifications can change; check the linked Apple documentation before delivery.

To report an error, email hi@damastechnologies.com with the guide URL, the statement in question, and a source or reproduction detail. Corrections are reviewed through the same pull-request process.

What these guides can establish

Technical checks can help produce crawlable pages and correctly sized files. They do not guarantee Google indexing, search rankings, rich results, conversion improvements, or Apple approval. Design recommendations should be tested against the creator’s own artwork and audience.
