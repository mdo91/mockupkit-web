MockupKitGrowthResearch

Operated by Damas Technologies

An owner-operated research and editorial tool for Mockup Kit’s app-creator guides. Authorized operators connect their own Google accounts; there is no public registration.

How research data is handled

What the tool does

  • Reads Search Console performance for the Mockup Kit website to understand useful reader questions and existing coverage.
  • Requests Google Ads Keyword Planner ideas and historical keyword metrics. Its permitted operations do not create campaigns, change billing, or spend on ads.
  • Supplies selected research to a managed ChatGPT subscription session to prepare concise guide drafts. A human approves the current article revision before automatic publication.

Separate from the Mac app

This research connection serves the website’s editorial workflow. The native Mockup Kit app’s image composition and export continue locally under the Mac app privacy policy.

Questions or access-removal requests: hi@damastechnologies.com.
